I'm making a browser game and I need help finding its unique hook: the one mechanic that makes it exciting and different from other roguelike shooters. Read the context, then give me ideas.

## The game (working title UNLISTED)

A 3D third-person roguelike shooter with extraction, played in the browser. The camera is over the shoulder; you aim with the mouse or a controller. Built in plain JavaScript and three.js, with low-poly art (Quaternius and Kenney models) under neon lighting. It's built by one person working with an AI coding assistant, so ideas need to be buildable in code without new hand-made art or animation.

**Setting:** the Meridian Spire, a sealed 400-floor corporate megatower. Seven years ago the company's management AI, WARDEN, was given control of the tower's machines and never gave it back. The executives sealed themselves at the top, while the machines keep running factories and meeting quotas for customers who no longer exist. People who get in the way of a quota are deleted from the registry and become "the Unlisted": they can't buy air credits, open doors or legally exist. Under the foundations is "the Hollow", an ancient machine intelligence. WARDEN feeds people to it ("intake"), and they come back as husks that repeat the last thing they did. The tone is bleak corporate dystopia, told by tired people with dry humour.

**You:** Rook, a runner for the Unlisted resistance.

## The loop as it stands

1. **Hub, Mercy Deck:** a walkable neon market. You buy guns, armour and plug-ins (implants), take jobs from a board, talk to characters and invest in the deck.
2. **Go out** by freight lift on one of three kinds of run:
   - **Story chapters (8):** open city districts with an objective (steal a case, kill a boss, overload relays).
   - **Side jobs.**
   - **The Descent:** 3 floors of rooms that seal until cleared, with a shop room and a floor boss.
3. **On a run:**
   - **Enemies:** gangs, robots and Hollow creatures. They come in waves triggered by walking into areas or grabbing the objective, and a lockdown timer raises the danger.
   - **Elite variants:** shielded, swift, or volatile (explodes on death).
   - **Relics:** pickups, with some pairs combining into a synergy.
   - **A black-market uplink** on each level offers deals: power now for a cost.
   - **Intake logs:** findable lore entries.
   - **An echo:** after one chapter, a copy of you trails behind and repeats your shots a few seconds late.
4. **Extract or die:**
   - **Extract:** keep the loot and get paid.
   - **Die:** lose the run and the kit you wore. Credits, unlocked guns, plug-ins and story progress stay. Your name goes on a memorial wall, and whoever killed you wears your kit as a bounty target.
5. **Also in the game:**
   - Dodge roll, jump and sprint.
   - Laser-bolt guns: revolver, rifle, shotgun, SMG and energy weapons.
   - A throwable pulse.
   - A buyable robot companion behind a side-quest line.
   - Synthesized music.

## The problem

It plays fine, but it doesn't have a hook. It reads as "a competent extraction shooter with roguelike bits". Every system above exists in other games. I want one or two central ideas that make it memorable and make each run feel different and exciting. They should come out of the setting if possible: a deleted person in a machine-run tower, an AI that treats people as stock, an ancient machine that copies people.

## What I want from you

Give me 8 to 10 hook ideas. For each:
- **The hook in one sentence**, the way it would be pitched on a store page.
- **How it plays:** what the player does differently, moment to moment and run to run.
- **Why it fits the setting.**
- **Which existing systems it replaces or upgrades.** I'd rather deepen what's there than pile on more.
- **Build cost:** small, medium or large, given that it's code-only, browser, three.js, solo dev, and no new animation.
- **The risk:** how it could be boring, unfair or confusing.

Then pick your top 2 and say why. Push back if you think the core loop itself is the problem. Ideas I've already got that you can build on or beat: the echo that copies you, being "unlisted" (invisible to systems), the Hollow's intake turning people into husks, and the killer wearing your gear.
