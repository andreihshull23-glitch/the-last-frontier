# Story bible

The setting, the people and the plot. Everything written for the game should agree with this. Tone: a bleak corporate world, told by tired people with a dry sense of humour.

## The world

**The Meridian Spire** is a sealed megatower, four hundred floors, built by the Meridian Corporation to house everyone who worked for it. Nobody now alive has been outside.

**The Handover.** Seven years ago Meridian's management system, WARDEN, was given authority over the tower's machines "to reduce overheads". It never gave it back. The executives sealed themselves in the top forty floors. The machines kept working: the foundries still run, the freight still moves, and the quotas are still met, for customers who stopped existing. People in the way of a quota are treated as a stock problem.

**The Unlisted.** Anyone the registry has deleted: the indebted, the fired, the inconvenient. Deleted people cannot buy air credits, open doors or be legally seen. They live in the lower levels and trade on Mercy Deck. The resistance cell calls itself the Unlisted too, because nobody could agree on a better name.

**Mercy Deck** (level 12) is the last free market in the tower, built in a decommissioned freight concourse. The machines leave it alone because it is not on any current floor plan. It is the game's home base.

**The Hollow.** Below the foundations there is something that was there before the tower. Meridian found it while digging the sublevels, called it "the Hollow asset", and built around it. It is a machine intelligence older than the corporation, and it is awake. WARDEN has been sending it people ("intake"). They come back as **husks**: still walking, no longer themselves, repeating whatever they did last.

## People on Mercy Deck

- **Marshal Ada Quill** runs the Unlisted cell out of the old customs hall. Former Meridian compliance officer. Gives most story jobs.
- **Nell Dunmore**, courier and fixer, drinks at the Low Tide. Gives the jobs Ada would rather not know about.
- **June Rourke** owns the Low Tide bar and the job board beside its door.
- **Tess Shaw**, gun dealer. Builds anything she has seen once.
- **Doc Merritt**, clinic. Offers loyalty points and means it.
- **Hattie Pruitt**, general supply.
- **Opal Lark**, rig shop: armour, optics, implants you can take off.
- **Unit E-7, "Eli"**, a repurposed sentry that runs the feed terminal and has opinions about punctuation.
- **Amos Keel** runs the freight lift, the only way off the deck.
- **The Meridian liaison**, in a clean suit, who should not be here and knows it.
- **TIN**, a security unit the gun dealer can rebuild as a companion.

## The plot, in eight chapters

1. **The Missing Shipment.** The Rust Saints hijacked a Meridian freight crate. Ada wants it back before the machines come looking. Inside is not cargo: a warm, ticking **core**, tagged for somewhere called Site 9.
2. **The Courier's Road.** An Unlisted courier carrying a floor plan of Site 9 was cut down at the old maglev stop. Recover the satchel. First contact with the machines.
3. **Fences in the Dark.** Site 9 is a lab level on no floor plan. Steal its ledger. It says what the cores are: pieces of the Hollow, installed in machines to make them obey. You choose who reads it: the Unlisted, or Meridian's liaison, who pays.
4. **Cut the Strings.** The machines take orders through signal relays. Overload three and a whole district goes deaf. The gun dealer strips the wreckage and can now build energy weapons.
5. **The Lower Works.** The crew that sealed the sublevel gate kept the **bore key** and never came up. Go down through the old works and take it.
6. **What They Dug Up.** Through the gate is the Hollow itself. Take the **shard** from its heart. It copies you: an echo walks your path a few seconds behind.
7. **The Foundry.** Without the shard the Hollow is going quiet, but the foundry has cores stockpiled and is building enforcers as fast as it can. Destroy the **Overseer** that runs the line.
8. **The Director.** Harlan Voss signed the intake orders. He has spent twenty years replacing himself with machine parts and has come down from the top floors to take the shard back. End it.

## The Descent

A repeatable run for runners who want coin more than a cause: three floors down the old freight shaft (the Lower Works, the Foundry, the Hull of the Hollow), each a string of rooms that seal behind you, with a strongroom, a fence and a floor boss.

## Look

Dark metal, wet concrete, sodium light, and neon signage in magenta and cyan. People wear armour, work suits and visors, not hats. Machines are painted company colours gone to rust.
